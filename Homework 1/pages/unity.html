Unity Overview

Unity is one of the most widely used game engines, supporting cross-platform for 2D and 3D game development. It also has a massive Asset Store with plenty of free assets that you can use to make games. Due to it's popularity and tenure, it also makes it a great choice for beginners due to it having a wide variety of tutorials and guides.

Unity excells with 2D projects and still works amazing for 3D projects, but it can struggle when it comes to incredibly graphically intensive games that require significant hardware. Unity also takes a fee for large scale projects unless you pay for a expensive monthly fee which increases based on how much revenue the game has made. The future for the game engine is also a little shaky as some recent changes has made it less community focused, shifted due to shareholders to be more for-profit then previous endeavours.
